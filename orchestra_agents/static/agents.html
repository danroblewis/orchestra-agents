Who is in this project and what they are for; the Dashboard shows what they are doing right now. A role is a job description. An agent is an instance of roles in a team and is what runs: continuous agents are processes that are always up and wake on events, scheduled agents run on an interval or a daily time, ad hoc agents get a fresh session each time their lead briefs them or a subscribed event fires. A team has one lead, responsibilities it keeps true, access, and a space in the repository. All of it is created, paused, reset or removed in conversation with the Supervisor and stored under .orchestra/; this page reads. Each agent's row says what wakes it and what it emits; who publishes and who listens for what, as observed on the bus, is the Machines page.
